# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

One person: the owner, who trains with weights several times a week and logs every session on his own iPhone. Two scenes:

- **At the gym**, between sets: phone in one hand or on the bench, hands chalky or sweaty, 60 to 180 seconds of rest, attention split with the next set. He needs to confirm what he just lifted, see what comes next and know when rest is over.
- **Away from the gym**, usually at home: building programs, days and exercises, reading history and progress, exporting backups.

There are no other users, no accounts and no audience to persuade.

## Product Purpose

A personal training log, installed from the iPhone Home Screen as a PWA, that plans workouts and records them set by set. It exists so that logging never slows down a workout: when a set repeats last time, recording it takes one tap. Success is a complete workout logged without friction, offline, and a history that is never lost and shows real progress over months.

## Positioning

Built around one lifter's own data and habits, not a market: local-first with no account, prefilled from his own last session, a rest timer that survives iOS suspending the app (computed from a stored end time, backed by a push notification), and sessions stored as immutable snapshots so editing a program never rewrites the past.

## Operating Context

- Installed to the Home Screen and launched standalone on iOS Safari (WebKit); portrait only; Italian only; kilograms only.
- Gym use: one-handed, fast, glanceable at arm's length, often under bright overhead light; the screen stays on through Wake Lock during a workout.
- Rest timer: in-app bar plus a Web Push notification sent by a Cloudflare Worker when the app is in the background.
- Planning use: longer, calmer sessions editing programs and reading charts.
- Backup is a core ritual, not an extra: data lives only in IndexedDB on the device and disappears if the Home Screen icon is removed.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Shipped (phases 0 to 3 of `docs/SPEC.md`): exercise library, programs with days, day rotation, workout mode with prefill and progression suggestion, supersets, warm-up sets, plate calculator, rest timer with push, history with editing, personal records, exercise charts, weekly sets per muscle group, consistency calendar, bodyweight, backup export and import, settings.
- Terminology is fixed by SPEC §2: Esercizio, Gruppo muscolare, Programma, Giornata, Sessione, Serie di lavoro, Riscaldamento, Recupero, e1RM, Volume.
- iOS constraints from CLAUDE.md bind every design: no vibration, inputs at least 16px, `viewport-fit=cover` with safe-area insets, JavaScript suspended in background, Wake Lock re-acquired on return, push only in standalone mode, never reload during a workout.
- Data rules bind every design: history is never lost, sessions are snapshots, every workout change is written to IndexedDB immediately.
- Stack is fixed (Vite, React, TypeScript, Tailwind v4 tokens in `@theme`, Dexie, Recharts in lazy routes, lucide-react). New dependencies need the owner's approval; for the redesign he approved self-hosted fonts and one animation library.
- Out of scope (SPEC §14): accounts, sync, social, Health and Apple Watch, multiple users, pounds, other languages.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: "Palestra" (provisional, SPEC §10).
- Olympic plate colour semantics, each with one meaning and never decoration: blue = primary action and current set, yellow = personal records, green = rest finished and completed sets, red = destructive actions. The plate calculator shows real plate colours (25 red, 20 blue, 15 yellow, 10 green, 5 white, 2.5 and 1.25 steel). Exact hues may be re-tuned; the mapping may not.
- Copy rules from SPEC §11.4: Italian, sentence case, no all-caps labels, active verbs that say what happens, the same action has the same name everywhere, specific errors that point to the fix, empty states as invitations, names from the user's point of view.
- Number formatting: `Intl.NumberFormat('it-IT')`, comma as decimal separator, sets separated by " / " (`80×10 / 80×9 / 77,5×8`).

## Evidence on Hand

- The owner's own training data in IndexedDB; the built-in exercise library in SPEC §13 (`src/db/seed.ts`).
- App icons in `public/` (`icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png`, `icon-maskable-512.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png`), generated by `scripts/gen-icons.ts`.
- There are no testimonials, users, press or marketing claims, and none should be invented.

## Product Principles

1. **The gym moment wins.** In workout mode, weight, reps and rest time are the largest things on screen and every action sits in the thumb zone; anything that slows a set down is a defect, however good it looks.
2. **One tap when nothing changed.** Prefill, sensible defaults and confirmation flows exist to keep the repeated set to a single tap.
3. **History is sacred.** Nothing in the interface may suggest that past sessions can be lost or silently changed; destructive actions are explicit and confirmed.
4. **Local-first, always available.** Every screen works offline and survives iOS closing the app mid-workout.
5. **Personal, not social.** No streak pressure, leaderboards or gamified nagging; progress is shown as the owner's own numbers.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Touch targets at least 44pt, 56pt in workout mode; one-handed reach for primary actions.
- Glanceable at arm's length under bright gym light: high contrast for numbers and timer.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` respected for all motion; no information carried by colour alone (plate colours always pair with text or an icon).
